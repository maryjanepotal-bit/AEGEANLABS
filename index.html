Harmony

Multi-Asset Liquidity Bridge

A liquidity bridge engineered with an in-flight Validation Tool that stops toxic flow before execution, and a Smart Aggregator that keeps your margin accounts safe at every LP.

  • Blocks latency arbitrage and stale-price hits before they touch your book
  • Routes around LP limits before they hit — your accounts stay healthy, your team stops firefighting

  • Candlestick P&L, exposure heatmaps, and client behavior scoring
